Radiant Capital Recovery Bounty Program - The Program incentivizes individuals, organizations, exchanges, security researchers, ethical hackers, and other third parties to assist in locating, freezing, and recovering misappropriated funds.

Eligible contributors can receive bounties of up to 20% for independent recoveries or 5% for platform-assisted recoveries, subject to KYC/KYB and compliance review.

- You agree to keep all investigative details **confidential** unless authorized.
